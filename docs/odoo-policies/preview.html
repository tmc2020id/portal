Data Deletion

How to delete your data

How to ask TMC to delete your personal data, including data we received through Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Google or other platforms you connected to our services.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Your right to deletion

You can ask us to delete your personal data at any time. Requests are free, and we confirm when the deletion is done.

How to send a request

1. Contact us

Email sales@tmcsmart.shop with the subject “Data deletion request”, or send “Delete my data” on WhatsApp to +963 965 445 580.

2. Tell us who you are

Include your name and the phone number, email or social media account you used with us, so we can find your data.

3. Verification

We may send a one-time code to your phone or email to make sure the request comes from you.

4. Confirmation

We confirm receipt within 3 working days, complete the deletion within 30 days, and send you a confirmation.

Facebook, Instagram and other platforms

Remove the app

On Facebook, open Settings & privacy › Settings › Apps and websites, find the TMC SMART app and choose Remove. On other platforms, remove TMC SMART from your connected apps.

Then ask us to delete

Removing the app stops any new access. To delete the data we already received, send a request as described above and mention the platform.

What we delete and what we keep

What we delete

Your contact details, customer account, chat history, assistant conversations, and the access tokens and data we received from connected platforms.

What we must keep

Invoices and transaction records that accounting and legal obligations require us to keep. We keep only what is needed and delete it when that period ends.

كيف تعمل الخدمة

أرسل لنا اسم المنتج أو رابطه أو صورته، مع الكمية وأي تفاصيل. يتحقق فريقنا منه لدى موردينا ويعود إليك بالتوفر والسعر ومدة التسليم المتوقعة.

1. أرسل طلبك

أخبرنا بما تحتاجه: اسم المنتج أو رابطه أو صورته، والكمية.

2. نؤمّنه لك

نتحقق من التوفر والسعر لدى موردينا.

3. أكّد واستلم

نؤكد معك السعر ومدة التسليم، ثم نسلّمك الطلب.

ماذا يمكنني أن أطلب؟

أي منتج تقني غير متوفر في المتجر. تعتمد مدة التسليم على المنتج والمورد، ويخبرك فريقنا بالمدة المتوقعة قبل أن تؤكد.
